# Rise Sleep Health

A modern, responsive healthcare website for **Rise Sleep Clinics**, focused on sleep health, sleep apnoea diagnosis, CPAP therapy, oral appliance therapy, sleep services, clinic information, and appointment enquiries.

## 🌐 Live Website

[View Live Website](https://pankajbusiness2sell.github.io/risesleephealth/index.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## 📌 About the Project

Rise Sleep Health is a frontend website designed for a sleep-health clinic. The website provides users with information about sleep apnoea, available treatments, CPAP machines and masks, clinic locations, patient feedback, blog content, and appointment/contact options.

The website is designed to provide a clean and user-friendly experience for patients looking for sleep-health services.

## 📄 Main Sections

### Home

The homepage introduces Rise Sleep Clinics and highlights the company's sleep-health services.

### About Us

Provides information about Rise Sleep-Health Professionals and their patient-centred approach.

### Sleep Treatments

Information about:

* Sleep Apnoea Diagnosis
* CPAP Therapy
* Oral Appliance Therapy
* Specialist Sleep Services

### CPAP

Dedicated sections for CPAP machines, masks, therapy, and patient support.

### Clinics

Clinic information and locations including:

* Robina
* Hope Island
* Labrador
* Upper Mount Gravatt
* Coolangatta

### Sleep Assessment

Users can access a free online sleep assessment designed to help identify the likelihood of sleep apnoea.

### Testimonials

Patient/client feedback and reviews are displayed to build trust and credibility.

### Blog / News

The website contains recent sleep-health articles and educational content.

### Contact & Appointment

Users can submit their information to request an appointment or enquiry.
